<objective>
Discharge the two non-code obligations `47-VERIFICATION.md` and `47-REVIEW.md` attached to this
closure: the WR-01 dead-code finding, and the requirement-checkbox bookkeeping the worktree executors
could not perform.

Purpose: `typsphinx/writer.py::_resolve_entry_element()` is fully implemented, extensively
documented, and called ~15 times by `tests/test_entry_metadata_precedence.py` — and by zero
production call sites. `render_wrapper()` uses `_entry_element_value()` exclusively, and the dead
function's own docstring already records that its docname first-match semantics are "deliberately NOT
used for a wrapper's own title/author (D-08)". A superseded implementation with a green test suite is
worse than no coverage: it reports confidence in a code path no build executes. Separately,
`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` still shows six genuinely-satisfied IDs as unchecked purely because the
worktree executors could not reach `gsd-tools.cjs`, which leaves the next verification pass reading a
false starting state.

**Decision, made here rather than left as "consider": DELETE.** D-08 is locked and states that a
wrapper resolves its metadata positionally off its own entry, never through a docname search; the
docname-first-match route has no future consumer, and CONF-13 (which would introduce per-entry
template keys) reinforces per-entry positional reading rather than reviving a docname search.
Retention-with-a-docstring was the reviewer's alternative and is rejected: an unreferenced function
in a 483-line module is exactly the thing a future maintainer assumes is load-bearing.

Output: the dead resolver gone, its surviving semantics retargeted onto the live resolver, its
docname-search-only assertions deleted with recorded rationale, and six requirement checkboxes
corrected.
</objective>
