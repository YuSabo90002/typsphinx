<objective>
Widen `TemplateEngine`'s resolution so the resolved template PATH is recoverable, through the existing
single priority walk. This is the one piece of Phase 53 with no runtime consumer in Phase 53 itself —
Phase 54's `<outdir>/_template/<key>/` bundle copy needs "the resolved template's parent directory"
and cannot get it from `resolve_template()` today, which discards the concrete path at all three
branches.

The discretion CONTEXT left open resolves in favour of a new field on `TemplateResolution` rather than
a separate `resolve_template_path()` method: RESEARCH Q2 measured that `TemplateResolution(` is
constructed at exactly three sites (template_engine.py:311, 324, 336), all inside `resolve_template()`
itself, and no test constructs it directly — so widening carries zero call-site migration cost, while
every "separate method" formulation either duplicates the three-branch walk (which the class's own
docstring at template_engine.py:290-295 forbids) or is a thin wrapper around the widened dataclass
anyway.

Purpose: make the resolved path available to Phase 54 without ever creating a second lookup.
Output: `TemplateResolution`'s new field, populated at all three priorities, with additive assertions.
</objective>
