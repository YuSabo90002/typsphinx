  <action>
Replace both delegations with their own leaf-emission bodies, per contract §5.2.

`visit_literal_strong` emits the bold-monospace form: the node's text escaped by the shared
string-literal escaping helper, wrapped in Typst's bold call around its monospace call.
`visit_literal_emphasis` emits the italic-monospace form: the same escaped text wrapped in Typst's
italic call around its monospace call. Both mirror the inline-literal handler's leaf idiom exactly —
paragraph separator, the concat-separator fallback with its list-item newline, the emission with the
markup-mode prefix, the mark-content fallback, then skip descent.

Three constraints:

1. **Use the shared string-literal escaping helper, `escape_typst_string`.** Do NOT reuse Phase 37's
   `_emit_signature_leaf_wrapper` or `_escape_signature_text`. They already produce the wrapper-around-
   monospace shape, which makes them the tempting shortcut, but `_escape_signature_text`
   unconditionally injects the SIG-07 break opportunity after every dot. No FLD requirement or CONTEXT
   decision authorizes that in a field body, field bodies are not measured to overflow the way dotted
   signature qualnames are, and adding it here smuggles unmeasured behaviour in under an unrelated
   refactor.
2. **No special-casing on the parent node.** A `:type:` that resolves as a cross-reference produces this
   node nested inside a reference, which the translator renders as a link call. The generic leaf body
   composes correctly there because the link's body argument is just a content value — the same reason
   the signature family's own resolved-xref rule already works. An `isinstance` check on the parent
   would be dead weight that hides the real reason it works.
3. **Reach monospace only through Typst's monospace primitive.** Never through a font-family selection:
   naming a font silently shadows this project's Japanese build's CJK fallback, and Typst reports that
   as neither a warning nor an error.

Leave `depart_literal_strong` and `depart_literal_emphasis` in place as stubs — they become unreachable
once the visit handlers skip descent, exactly as the inline-literal handler's own depart already is.
Say that in their docstrings rather than deleting them; the docutils dispatcher contract still wants
them present.

Whether the two bodies are verbatim copies of each other or share a small private helper is yours under
D-12. Phase 36's deliberate triplication is the cited precedent and either choice is acceptable. If you
choose the helper, name it in the SUMMARY as a new symbol.

Do not fold in Phase 36 D-02's shared-attribute leak repair — that is Phase 39's deferred work. Just do
not make it worse.

Touch nothing else: not the field-list or field-body handlers plan 38-06 landed, not the body wrapper
plan 38-05 landed, not the signature family.
