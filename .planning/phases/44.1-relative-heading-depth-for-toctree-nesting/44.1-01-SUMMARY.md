# Phase 44.1 Plan 01: Two-Part Relative Heading Depth Repair Summary

**`visit_title` now emits Typst's relative `depth:` argument and `visit_toctree` now emits a
context-relative `heading.offset + 1` increment, so nested toctree documents resolve one level
deeper than their parent and nested toctrees compose — proven end to end by a new
`typst.query(field="level", root=...)` render gate against a real `sphinx-build -> typst.compile()`
pipeline.**

## Accomplishments

- Wrote and recorded RED for a new permanent render gate
  (`tests/test_heading_depth_render_gate.py`) that measures the *resolved* heading level of a
  compiled multi-document project via `typst.query(..., field="level", root=<build_dir>)` — a new
  verification technique for this suite (every prior render gate ended in pypdf text extraction).
- Landed D-07 part 1: `visit_title` emits `heading(depth: ...)` instead of `heading(level: ...)`
  at both of its emission sites; the pre-existing clamp survives with a rewritten, non-`D-06`
  rationale.

  instead of the absolute `set heading(offset: 1)`, so nested toctree scopes accumulate.
- Proved D-07's necessity empirically rather than assuming it: measured that part 1 alone passes
  SC#1 but leaves SC#2 failing (the grandchild stuck at the child's level), and only both parts
  together pass the gate's full four-test suite.
- Ran the full suite post-fix and split the 54 failures by measured root cause: 45 are a
  pre-existing NixOS `uv`-subprocess sandbox limitation (confirmed identical at the pre-fix anchor
  commit via a throwaway detached worktree), 9 are real assertions this fix invalidates — handed

  44.1-CONTEXT.md D-04's named addendum.
