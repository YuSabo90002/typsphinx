**Restated the `typst_authors` precedence rule over the assignment target `params["authors"]` at
all 9 sites that constrain it, backed by an AST writer enumeration and a mechanically generated
18-cell survival matrix that a rule one predicate short of the code cannot pass.**

## Accomplishments

- AST-based writer enumeration (`tests/test_params_authors_writers.py`) proves exactly three
  sites in `map_parameters()` can write `params["authors"]`, with the mapping loop's
  `params[template_key] = value` as the sole overwriter — grounding every other claim in this
  plan in the code's own structure rather than a hand-derived config-shape or source-key list.
- A generated 18-cell survival matrix (`SOURCE_KEYS x TARGET_KEYS x {present, absent}`) closes
  the two previously zero-coverage shapes: a non-author source (`project`, `release`) routed
  into `"authors"` destroys the seed with a non-author value, and `"author"` routed to a target
  other than `"authors"` (`doc_authors`, `writer`) keeps the seed and lands both values.
- Restated the rule identically at all 9 sites that constrain it: `configuration.rst`'s
  Precedence paragraph and item-4 bullet, `template_engine.py`'s D-05 comment (comment-only —
  `ast.dump` equality proven against `GAP_BASE`), five test-file sites, and
  `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`'s `CONF-10` entry (diff confined to that bullet).
- `44.2-GATE-EVIDENCE-05.md` grades every one of those 9 sentences ACCURATE against the
  generated row table with a named falsification-attempt row per sentence, and shows the
  specific row (`{"project": "authors"}`) that falsifies both `44.2-REVIEW.md` WR-01's and
  `44.2-VERIFICATION.md`'s suggested wordings.

## Decisions Made

- Enumerated the search set from the assignment target (`ast.walk` over `map_parameters()`'s
  `params[...]` writes) rather than from the mapping's source key, breaking the pattern that
  produced two prior falsified rounds.
- Deliberately deviated from `44.2-PATTERNS.md`'s discrete-named-function convention for the new
  module's survival matrix, using `@pytest.mark.parametrize` over a mechanically generated cross
  product — recorded as a deliberate deviation in the module's own docstring, since a
  hand-enumerated row set is exactly the failure mode being closed.
- `_seed_survives()` (the matrix's expected-value predicate) calls no production entry point, so
  the matrix cannot become a tautology that would have passed for either of the two already-
  falsified rules.
