## 1. The three mapping-resolution branches (`typsphinx/template_engine.py:235-243`)

`TemplateEngine.__init__` resolves `self.parameter_mapping` as: an explicitly-passed
`parameter_mapping` wins verbatim (it may or may not carry `"author"`); otherwise, if
`self.typst_package` is truthy the mapping becomes an EMPTY dict; otherwise it is a copy of
`DEFAULT_PARAMETER_MAPPING`, which is `{"project": "title", "author": "authors", "release": "date"}`
and therefore DOES map `"author"` (`template_engine.py:199-203`).

## 2. The overwrite condition (`typsphinx/template_engine.py:457-472`)

`map_parameters()` seeds `params["authors"]` from `self.typst_authors` first, then runs
`for sphinx_key, template_key in self.parameter_mapping.items(): if sphinx_key in sphinx_metadata:`
and assigns `params[template_key]`. There is no `self.typst_package` conditional anywhere in the
method. The seed is therefore overwritten exactly when BOTH hold: `"author"` is an active key in
`self.parameter_mapping`, AND `"author"` is present in the passed `sphinx_metadata`.
