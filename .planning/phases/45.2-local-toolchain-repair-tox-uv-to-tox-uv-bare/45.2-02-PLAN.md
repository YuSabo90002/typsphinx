<objective>
Replace the `tox-uv` meta package with `tox-uv-bare` and make `tox.ini`'s four broken environments
declare their tooling the way the three working ones already do — then prove the whole chain end to
end by running a real `tox` environment to completion.

Purpose: two defects, one plan. Defect ① is the `tox-uv` meta package dragging in the PyPI `uv`
wheel, whose generic-linux ELF at `.venv/bin/uv` NixOS cannot exec — reached by `tox-uv` through
`uv.find_uv_bin()`, which searches `.venv/bin` first and reads no environment variable. Defect ② is
`runner = uv-venv-lock-runner` provisioning solely from `uv.lock` via `uv sync`, which can install
only what `pyproject.toml` declares — so the `deps =` lists on `[testenv]`, `[testenv:lint]`,
`[testenv:type]` and `[testenv:cov]` are inert and those environments have never contained their
own tools.

Output: an edited `pyproject.toml` dev extra, an edited `tox.ini`, a regenerated `uv.lock`, and a
transcript of `tox -e type` completing successfully with no environment override.
</objective>
