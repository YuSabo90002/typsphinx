<objective>
Read the raw RTD build log for the PDF-enabling commit, record the decisive excerpt verbatim, and make
the Branch A / Branch B selection an explicit decision gated on that recorded evidence.

Purpose: this is the phase's one real fork, and the milestone's one genuinely open empirical unknown —
whether `typst.compile()` can reach `packages.typst.org` from inside RTD's build sandbox. No
documentation source resolves it; only a real build log does. D-07 requires the verdict to be a
recorded excerpt rather than an inference, precisely because both outcomes are plausible and the
convenient one is not necessarily the true one. This plan also closes RESEARCH assumptions A1 (the apt
package name) and A2 (temp-directory writability) from the same log, and records whether RTD's download
menu actually offers a PDF.

Output: `29-VERIFICATION.md` § SC#2 with the verbatim excerpt and the whole-log search for LaTeX-toolchain
lines, plus § Branch Decision naming `branch-a` or `branch-b` and citing the excerpt.
</objective>
