  record names what would close the gap: a direct owner look at page 5 (both the overall highest-density
  page in the sample and 単's carrier).
- Confirmed `tofu` on pages 2 and 3 is mapped onto explanation (a) (glyph genuinely absent), reasoned
  explicitly: a broken `ToUnicode` CMap (explanation (b)) corrupts text extraction only — the glyph
  outline a PDF viewer paints is addressed by glyph ID, not through the CMap — so explanation (b) cannot
  itself produce a visible tofu substitution. Only explanation (a) is consistent with what the owner
  directly observed on the two confirmed pages.
- The zen-twilight/NixOS viewer caveat is recorded as raising rather than lowering confidence in the two
  confirmed `tofu` results: Zen Browser's default in-browser PDF viewer is Firefox's `pdf.js`, which does
  its own font substitution — a tofu result surviving through a substitution-capable viewer is stronger
  evidence for genuine glyph absence than an `ok` result from the same viewer would have been for correct
  rendering.
