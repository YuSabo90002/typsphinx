  session. There was never a milestone-branch value selectable there. The milestone-branch pin instead
  lives in that repository's own `.gitmodules` `branch` field (Handoffs item 5).

  reply did not include the three raw-log excerpts the `<resume-signal>` asked for. This executor fetched
