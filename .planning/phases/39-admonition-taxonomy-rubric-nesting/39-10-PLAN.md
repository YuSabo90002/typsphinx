<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `pluralization`. One `error()` function becomes three distinct clue functions for the red
family.

**The noun that is now primary.** It is no longer "the red bucket" (one function). It is **the red
family** — a SET of clue functions that share a semantic group and a hue range, whose members are
distinguished by icon shape rather than by being the same call. The evidence that the general noun is
the right primary is that Sphinx itself already models it that way: `sphinx.sty:853-860` puts
attention, danger and error in one colour bucket while `sphinx.sty:933-943` gives each a distinct
icon. The folded model could not express that at all; the family model expresses both the grouping
and the per-type distinction.

**Decision: `promote`.** The general representation — "a bucket is a FAMILY of one or more clue
functions sharing a hue range" — becomes primary, and the old specific representation — "a bucket is
exactly one clue function" — is demoted to the case that happens to hold for the note, success and
warning groups. This is the standard correct move on a generalization, and it is chosen over
`add-alongside` deliberately: adding a second, parallel notion of "red family" beside the existing
"bucket = one function" rule would leave the codebase with two competing taxonomies and no rule for
which one a future type is filed under.

**What promotion costs, and why it is still right.** D-01's rule that a bucket is expressed as a
function NAME and never as a colour argument survives untouched — promotion changes the cardinality
of the mapping, not its representation. The one thing that does not survive is ADM-02's phrasing,
which encoded the singular assumption directly into the requirement text; that is exactly why this
plan restates it rather than reinterpreting it.

**Invariant recommended, and taken.** Plan 39-09 authors
`test_red_family_types_route_to_distinct_clue_functions`, which encodes the generalized intent: every
red-family admonition type routes to a clue function drawn from a hand-transcribed red-family set, no
two share a function, and each carries its `sphinx.locale.admonitionlabels` title. A future phase
cannot silently reintroduce the singular assumption without turning that test red. Recorded here as a
suggestion that was accepted rather than left optional.

**Not accepted debt.** Because the disposition is `promote` rather than `add-alongside`, there is no
debt to record and nothing that would later force a promote — the promote is happening now.
</assumption_delta_decision>
