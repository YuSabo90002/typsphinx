<objective>
Prove the gate RED against a genuinely restored unfixed tree, transcribe the refusal verbatim, and
bind the 9 must-keep-passing shapes to committed goldens captured from that same unfixed tree.

Purpose: a gate observed only green is tautological - it is exactly the failure mode that let this
defect ship in 0.9.0 past nine string-level image tests. This plan is where "the fix works" becomes
"the fix was proven to have been necessary", and where the one measured non-byte-identical PASS shape
is pinned to an exact committed delta rather than waived.

Output: `62-RED-EVIDENCE.md` carrying the full RED transcript and the positive control; ten committed
golden files; a golden-comparison test class; and the phase's own todo closed against the extended
matrix.
</objective>
