Worktree isolation is the standing execution mode (`CLAUDE.md` § "Worktree-isolated execution";
ROADMAP milestone constraint 12). It is mandatory and not conditional on parallelism. Before running
ANY command in this plan, provision this worktree's own virtual environment:

`env -u VIRTUAL_ENV -u UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT uv sync --extra dev`

Then prefix every subsequent command with `uv run`.

**The `docs` extra is deliberately NOT synced into this worktree's venv.** This is load-bearing, not
an omission. `myst_parser` lives in the `docs` extra only, and four tests are gated on importing it;
the carried-in full-suite baseline of 1543 passed / 5 skipped was measured in exactly this
`--extra dev` environment, with those four among the five skips. Adding the `docs` extra here would
turn four skips into passes and make this plan's count incomparable to the baseline it must be
checked against. D-11's own proof already ran under the `docs` extra, in plan 63-01 Task 3; the two
measurements are taken in different environments on purpose, and `63-GREEN-TREE-EVIDENCE.md` records
that split explicitly. The `docs-html` and `docs-pdf` tox environments declare `extras = docs`
themselves and are provisioned by `tox` into their own `.tox` environments, leaving this worktree's
`.venv` untouched — so running them does not violate this rule.
