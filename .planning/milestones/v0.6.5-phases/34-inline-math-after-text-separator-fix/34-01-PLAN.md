## Flagged assumptions (spec-less edge-probe fallback, §C)

No SPEC.md exists for this phase, so the four edge-coverage probes were resolved by the planner and
lifted into `must_haves.truths` above. All four are classified `covered` (a defensible acceptance
criterion exists for each); none were dismissed. Two carry a stated judgement worth reviewing:

1. **empty** — a genuinely empty math body (`:math:` with no content) is judged OUT of the input
   domain because docutils rejects empty interpreted text at parse time. The single-element case (a
   list item whose sole content is inline math) is covered instead.
2. **encoding** — the encoding contract is asserted only on the PDF text-extraction side (NFKC
   normalization). Byte-exactness of the emitted math payload itself is asserted structurally (the
   exact ``mi(`E=mc^2`)`` string appears in the `.typ`) rather than by a dedicated non-ASCII fixture
   construct: a CJK/Greek math body would make the pypdf assertion font-coverage-dependent in this
   sandbox, which would weaken the gate rather than strengthen it.
