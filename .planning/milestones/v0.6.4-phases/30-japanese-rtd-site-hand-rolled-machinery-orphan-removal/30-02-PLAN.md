<objective>
Delete the four hand-rolled language-switcher assets, trim `docs/source/conf.py` to the switcher
wiring and nothing else, and repair the one test that the trim would otherwise redden — all in a
single commit.

Purpose: this is the heart of I18N-02. The switcher is a stylesheet, a sidebar template, a
`page.html` theme override, a 180-line build script, and four `conf.py` blocks that bind them
together; Read the Docs' own flyout has replaced all of it, observed working in both directions by
Phase 30.1. The delicate part is not the deletion — it is the two things around it. `conf.py` is
shared byte-for-byte with a *second* repository's Read the Docs build, so the Phase 29 language seam
and Phase 30.1's font block must come through untouched, proven by hash rather than by inspection.
And `tests/test_readthedocs_config.py` asserts four times against the very dict being deleted, so it
travels in the same commit (milestone invariant #5 — the exact trap that reddened the suite in
Phase 27).

Output: four files deleted, two files modified, one atomic commit, suite green throughout.
</objective>
