<objective>
Remove the two unreachable root-level documents together with the tests that hard-assert their
existence, and remove the locale catalogs that Phase 30.1 relocated but did not delete.

Purpose: two things in the tree currently claim to be documentation but are not. `docs/usage.rst`
and the root `docs/installation.rst` sit outside `docs/source/`, are named by no toctree, and have
never appeared in the built site — 819 lines that a reader cannot reach and that a maintainer might
still edit, believing it ships (DOC-08, D-11). And `docs/locale/ja/`'s 26 tracked files are now a
second, unmaintained copy of catalogs that live and are built in `typsphinx-doc-translations`;
after Plan 01 removes the Makefile targets that used to update them, nothing in this repository
writes to them at all (STATE.md PD-01).

The risk in both halves is the same and it is not the deletion — it is what travels alongside it.
Four test functions across two files assert the orphans exist, so they must leave in the same
commit or the suite goes red (invariant #5, the Phase 27 trap). And a file named
`installation.rst` also exists under `docs/source/`, is toctree-live, and must not be touched.

Output: six paths removed, nothing modified, nothing created; full suite green afterwards.
</objective>
