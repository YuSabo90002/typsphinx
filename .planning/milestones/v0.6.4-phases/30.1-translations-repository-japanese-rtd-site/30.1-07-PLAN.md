<objective>
Collect the two open owner observations — H1 (does RTD's flyout widget actually offer the language
switch on both sites) and H2 (do 発 / 単 / 釈 render as real Han ideographs or as tofu) — against a
freshly re-downloaded, SHA-identified artifact, and record both answers verbatim.

Purpose: H2 is the discriminator the root-cause work in Plan 10 depends on. A NUL byte in extracted text
is equally consistent with a missing glyph and with a broken `ToUnicode` CMap over a glyph that paints
correctly; no command available here separates them, and the two point at completely different causes and
completely different fixes. Sequencing the owner's look first turns it from a postscript into an input.
H1 is collected in the same round because it needs the same person and the same browser, and because the
owner's previous reply contradicted the machine measurement in a way that has never been reconciled.

This plan is also the last safe moment for H2: Plans 08-09 advance the submodule pin, which rebuilds the
ja site and reflows the PDF. Every page number in Plan 06's sample is a property of the 95-page artifact
built from pin `dcc6a523...` and stops being valid the moment that pin moves.

Output: `30.1-EVIDENCE.md` sections identifying the re-downloaded artifact, recording the owner's two
answers verbatim, re-measuring the flyout's data source in the same session, and mapping the H2 answer
onto the explanation it supports — without marking any success criterion verified.

**Autonomy note:** `autonomous: false` because Task 2 is a blocking owner observation. Under
`workflow.human_verify_mode: end-of-phase` a `checkpoint:human-verify` is not available; this is modelled
as `checkpoint:human-action` for the same reason Plans 03 and 04 were — opening a browser widget and
judging rendered glyphs has no CLI or API substitute in this environment, and the answer must arrive
before Plan 10 can run rather than at the end of the phase.
</objective>
