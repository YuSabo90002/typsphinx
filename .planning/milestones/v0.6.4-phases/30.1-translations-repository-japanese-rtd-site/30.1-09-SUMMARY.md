`$GITHUB_OUTPUT` writes are silent by design. Rather than treat this as a blocker, both values were
derived deterministically from facts the run's own log *does* print (the preceding steps' echoed
submodule SHAs and `sphinx-intl`'s per-file change report), and independently cross-checked in Task 2
by reproducing the decide step's exact shell predicates against the pushed commit from a completely
separate fresh clone. Both derivations agree. This is recorded in detail in `30.1-EVIDENCE.md` so a
future re-run of this workflow does not need to rediscover the same limitation.
