# Phase 30.1 Plan 06: D-03 Japanese PDF Glyph Gate — Checks 1-3 Machine-Verified, an Anomaly Found, Check 4 Left Open Summary

Ran D-03's four-check Japanese-PDF glyph gate against the RTD-served `typsphinx-ja` PDF (95 pages,
downloaded live) and a same-source local `SPHINX_LANGUAGE=ja` rebuild from the pinned commit
(`dcc6a523fc2d16f3dae925e17d63a1a24318b6d7`, 94 pages) — and, along the way, discovered that D-02's
original 2026-07-26 local baseline was itself built against this repository's stale, git-tracked `.mo`
cache rather than a faithful reproduction of what the ja manifest's `rm -rf`-then-copy step forces on
every RTD build; corrected for that and found the RTD PDF's extracted text contains **10 literal NUL
bytes**, all replacing exactly **three ordinary kanji (発/単/釈)** across 7 pages — a specific,
machine-evidenced content anomaly, not the routine "text extraction can't see tofu" case D-14 warns
about. Checks 1-3 are machine-verified (page count 94 vs 95 — not the English-on-ja-path signature;
text NOT equal with full diagnostic attribution; `HanaMinA` carries the CJK-coverage verdict, again
matching Phase 29's English-PDF finding). Check 4 (the owner's visual confirmation) is recorded
`human_needed` with a 10-page sample — the plan's 4-page density-based floor plus 6 pages flagged by the
NUL finding as high-priority. **I18N-03 is explicitly NOT recorded as satisfied.** Closed with a
phase-wide consolidated criterion table (SC#1 verified, SC#2/SC#4 `human_needed`, SC#3 NOT MET, SC#5
verified-by-handoff) and confirmation that Plan 04's D-15 gate paragraph still stands.

## Decisions Made

- **Rebuilt the local baseline from the pinned SHA in a detached worktree, not from this worktree's
  HEAD.** The reconciliation step found a non-empty `git diff --stat` on `docs/` between the RTD ja
  build's pinned submodule SHA and this worktree's current HEAD (Plan 05's `contributing.rst` addition)
  — per the plan's explicit instruction, a divergent source meant the baseline had to be rebuilt at the
  pinned SHA before any comparison could be trusted as same-source.
- **Corrected the local baseline's `.mo`-freshness after the naive rebuild's exact match to D-02 turned
  out to be a red flag, not a confirmation.** The naive rebuild reused this repository's stale,
  git-tracked `.mo` binaries (only 3/13 catalogs recompiled by Sphinx's mtime check) and happened to
  match D-02's 2026-07-26 measurement byte-for-byte — but that match meant D-02's own baseline shared
  the same confound, not that the comparison was valid. Removed the `.mo` files and rebuilt, mirroring
  the RTD ja manifest's own `rm -rf typsphinx/docs/locale` step, and used that corrected build (which
  independently matches RTD's own 5-warning PDF-pass count) for all of checks 1-3.
- **Recorded check 2's NUL finding with full diagnostic weight rather than filing it as a routine
  tofu-blind-spot note.** The plan anticipated check 2 typically passing (text extraction being blind to
  glyph substitution per D-14) or, if it fails, being recorded with a verbatim diff. This run's failure
  is unusually specific — 10 NUL bytes, exactly 3 characters, fully attributed to source `.po` lines —
  and that specificity is recorded as raising the stakes of check 4, not as a curiosity to note in
  passing.
- **Widened check 4's page sample beyond the plan's 4-page density method**, adding the 6 NUL-flagged
  pages as Claude's Discretion (the plan explicitly leaves page selection to execution). The plan's
  "four or five pages" language is the acceptance-criteria floor, not a ceiling; the extra pages are
  earned by direct evidence from check 2, not added by taste.
- **Did not attempt to resolve which of the two NUL explanations (missing glyph vs. broken ToUnicode
  CMap) is correct.** Both are consistent with the extracted-text evidence and neither is
  extraction-distinguishable from the other; per this project's standing verification culture, this
  ambiguity is recorded and handed to check 4 rather than guessed at.

## Issues Encountered

**A genuine content-fidelity anomaly was found in the RTD-built Japanese PDF, not introduced by this
plan's own work.** Ten literal NUL bytes appear in the RTD PDF's extracted text, replacing exactly three
kanji (発/単/釈) across 7 pages — see Task 2's full attribution table in `30.1-EVIDENCE.md` § "SC#4 —
D-03 checks 1-3". This is recorded as evidence for check 4's owner review, not as something this plan
fixes: per D-03's explicit no-script/no-in-flight-patch instruction (reaffirming Phase 29 D-15), no code
under `typsphinx/`, `docs/`, or the RTD manifests was touched. If the owner's visual check on the 8
flagged pages confirms tofu/missing glyphs, the remediation (a different/additional CJK font in
`build.apt_packages`, or an upstream Typst/font-fallback issue) is out of this plan's scope and belongs
to a follow-up.

D-02's original 2026-07-26 local baseline measurement is now known to have been built against this
repository's stale, git-tracked `.mo` cache rather than a faithful reproduction of the RTD build process
— see "Decisions Made" above. This does not invalidate D-02's own recorded figures (they were accurate to
the build process actually exercised at the time), but any future reader comparing against D-02's 94-page
figure should know it was not built the same way the RTD ja project's own `.readthedocs.yaml` builds.
