<objective>
Land FLD-01 and FLD-02: give the field list its own indent step nested inside the description body's,
and make a single-value field body render inline with its label while the multi-value case keeps its
bulleted rendering and the docutils-collapsed case stays byte-identical.

Purpose: today a field list is not indented at all, and `:returns: Nothing at all.` renders its value on
the line below its label because a lone paragraph child is unconditionally wrapped in Typst's
block-level paragraph call. The measured consequence of fixing both, on this project's own docs, is
97 pages to 87.

Output: one focused change to `typsphinx/translator.py` plus the migrations the census assigns to this
plan. The gates already exist from plan 38-02.
</objective>

  <action>
Make a single-value field body render inline with its label.

Extend `visit_field_body`'s existing classification with a second case: a body whose children are
exactly one paragraph node. That case activates the SAME inline-concat context the existing all-inline
case uses — reuse `_in_field_body` and `_field_body_has_content`; do not invent a second concat
mechanism. A single-value field body is always exactly one paragraph; that was verified by reading
Sphinx's own field-building code, so the classification is exact rather than heuristic.

Add a matching branch to `visit_paragraph` and `depart_paragraph`, placed before the existing
block-wrapper emission, that skips the paragraph wrapper when that classification is active — mirroring
the list-item fast-path already in both methods. The paragraph's children then dispatch completely
unmodified through the existing inline-concat machinery. The root cause is that Typst's paragraph call
is intrinsically block-level and starts a new visual line regardless of any separator, so removing the
wrapper is the fix; adding a separator is not.

**The trap, which is the hard part of this task.** `depart_field`'s inter-field separator is gated on
the flag `depart_field_body` derives from the concat context. If the new classification simply sets that
flag, the separator fires between `Returns:`, `Return type:` and `Raises:` and puts all three on ONE
line — producing a zero interval where the measurement says each field should cost one paragraph
interval, and destroying the very rhythm this change exists to create. The separator must stay scoped to
the **docutils-collapsed** case only. That requires distinguishing "collapsed-inline" from
"single-paragraph-unwrapped": add the one attribute needed for the distinction and name it for the
distinction it makes. The research anti-pattern forbids a second **concat** mechanism, not a separator
discriminator.

Consecutive single-value fields must each occupy their own paragraph. Whatever mechanism achieves that
is yours under D-12; the observable requirement is contract §4.3 property 2 and it is asserted by the
gate from plan 38-02.

**Byte-identity controls.** The docutils-collapsed confval case must stay exactly as it renders today —
its two fields legitimately DO share one line via the inter-field separator. Both confval modules must
stay green with their assertions unmodified.

**D-13.** Do not touch the stray break at the head of each bulleted list item. It is emitted by the
list-item paragraph fast-path shared by every list in the document, an existing test already pins its
exact shape, and the disposition recorded at plan time is to leave it. Note that in the docstring of
whichever handler you touch nearest to it, so the next reader does not think it was overlooked.

Land this as its own commit.
  </action>
