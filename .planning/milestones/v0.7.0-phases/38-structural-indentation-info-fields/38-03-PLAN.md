conjunction of "the body wrapper's tokens are present" and "the count is still exactly 8" can fail
pre-phase for the right reason and fail again if the wrapper lands without marker propagation. That
conjunction is what makes D-10 verifiable instead of assumed.

`38-GATE-EVIDENCE-03.md`. This plan does NOT touch `typsphinx/` and does NOT change any existing
expectation's value.
