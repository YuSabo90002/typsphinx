<objective>
Replace `desc_signature`'s wrapper with the one composed `block`-plus-`par` form, introduce the shared
indent constant, and make every text-bearing descendant of a signature emit through the monospace
primitive — including the per-sub-part bold and italic treatments D-01 specifies.

Purpose: this is the change that turns a signature from a run of proportional bold text into typeset
code. It carries SIG-09 (page keep-together) and SIG-07 (hanging indent plus break opportunities) in a
single wrapper, and SIG-01/02/03/04 through the monospace flag plus three small handler bodies.

Output: one `typsphinx/translator.py` commit series. No test changes — every gate already exists from
Wave 1.
</objective>
