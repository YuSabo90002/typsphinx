<objective>
Make the two published asset examples describe what actually works under the bundle layout, and
prove both of them with one real build instead of one review.

Purpose: `advanced.rst` is wrong twice over — it tells the reader the template lands at the output
root under a basename that no longer exists, and consequently tells them to reference the asset by a
source-tree-shaped subpath that will not resolve. `templates.rst`'s closing note understates the
rule in the opposite direction, restricting bundle copying to custom local templates when the
built-in key is copied by the same rule. Extending one existing fixture proves both corrections.

Output: a `refs.bib` and a bibliography call in the existing relative-asset fixture; a destination
assertion and a prose-binding class; corrected prose on both pages.

Decision provenance: DOC-16 was left to Claude's discretion in `56-CONTEXT.md`, with the recorded
recommendation to extend `tests/fixtures/user_template_relative_asset_gate/` rather than author a new
project. That recommendation is followed here without departure — it is the only one of the three
available precedents that proves both published examples with one real compile and adds no Typst
Universe dependency. The colliding-destination refusal this plan deliberately declines to editorialize
about is D-05's shape #7, already published by plan 56-01 on `configuration.rst`; the no-exceptions
bundle rule the closing note must state is Phase 54's OUT-04.
</objective>
