3. `[("index",)]` → `-b typst` exits 0 and `index.typ` on disk becomes the wrapper with a
   self-referential `#include("index.typ")`; the docname's own translated content, sentinel and all,
   is destroyed (`grep -c` of the body sentinel = 0).

<assumption_delta_decision>
**Noun now primary:** *a usable `typst_documents` entry* — the entry shape that can produce a wrapper
file.

**Decision: `promote`.**

Rationale: before this plan, four sites each expressed their own ad-hoc notion of "an entry I can
use" — `not doc_tuple` in `TypstPDFBuilder.finish()`; `not entry or len(entry) < 2 or not
isinstance(entry[0], str)` in `_validate_output_path_collisions()`; `entry and entry[0] in docnames`
in `write()`'s D-07 report; `not entry or entry[0] != docname` in `_write_typst_files()`. The concept
was real and load-bearing but unnamed, and the four spellings drifted — that drift is the literal
cause of gap 2. Extracting `_is_usable_typst_documents_entry()` promotes the concept to a first-class
named predicate with exactly one owner and four consumers. This is a promote, not an add-alongside:
no site keeps its own private spelling of the predicate afterwards.

The `_collision_key()` normalization change is deliberately NOT part of this delta — it completes an
existing named concept ("the comparison-only collision key") rather than introducing a new noun, so
it warrants no separate promotion.
</assumption_delta_decision>

| T-47-11-01 | Tampering | `TypstBuilder._collision_key()` vs. the outdir-containment guard `_escapes_outdir()` | medium | mitigate | **The security question the planning brief asked, answered by measurement rather than assumed.** Adding `posixpath.normpath()` to the COMPARISON key cannot change, mask or bypass the containment check on the WRITE path, on three independent grounds. (a) *Separation:* `_collision_key()`'s return value is used only as a `dict` key inside `_validate_output_path_collisions()`; no write site consumes it.
