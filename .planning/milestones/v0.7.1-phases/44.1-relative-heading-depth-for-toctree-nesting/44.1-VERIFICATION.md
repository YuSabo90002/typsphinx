# Phase 44.1: Relative Heading Depth for Toctree Nesting — Verification Report

**Phase Goal:** A document reached through a toctree renders its headings one level deeper than its
parent, so the PDF outline nests instead of being flat. The repair has two parts: (1) `visit_title`
emits `depth:` instead of `level:`; (2) `visit_toctree` emits a context-relative offset increment
(`heading.offset + 1`) instead of an absolute assignment, so nested toctree scopes accumulate
(D-07).
