<objective>
Extend the auto-derived Typst `lang` to every non-package template route (CONF-12, D-I) by
narrowing `TemplateEngine.uses_bundled_default_template()` to its `typst_package` guard alone, and
remove the repository's own workaround for the asymmetry.

Purpose: CONF-07's D-06 withheld `lang` from an explicit `typst_template` on the grounds that such
a template might not declare it. D-A's published nine-parameter contract removes that rationale,
and the LaTeX-parity argument cuts against it — Sphinx's LaTeX builder derives `babel`/`polyglossia`
from `language` regardless of `latex_elements`. The strongest evidence that the asymmetry is a real
cost is in this repository: `docs/source/conf.py` imports the derivation helper from
`typsphinx.template_engine` and rebuilds `typst_elements["lang"]` by hand, with a comment saying
setting `typst_template` "silently drops that auto-derivation".

Output: the narrowed judgment, two inverted `typst_lang_gate` fixtures, the recorded pre-change
behaviour, and a clean `tox -e docs-pdf` with the workaround gone.
</objective>
